# Sarvottam Cables Ltd (SRVCABLE) — Research Brief

## Snapshot
Power-cables, wires and conductors manufacturer. Q1 FY27 revenue was ₹1,248 crore (+18.0% YoY), EBITDA ₹140 crore (+2.6%) and PAT ₹82 crore (+5.9%); EBITDA margin fell to 11.2% from 12.9%. Order book was ₹3,900 crore and exports were 21% of revenue. [S5]
The Bharuch plant is planned to start commercial production in Q3 FY27 and is described as adding about 30% to cable capacity. Management maintained FY27 revenue-growth guidance of 15–17%. [S5] [S4]

## Bull Case
- **Demand/backlog:** The ₹3,900 crore order book is up from ₹3,150 crore a year earlier, while management says power-transmission and data-centre demand remains strong. Data-centre cables are about 9% of domestic revenue, with management expecting that to double in two years. [S5] [S4]
- **Capacity + growth:** Bharuch adds about 30% to cable capacity and is on track for Q3 FY27 production; management continues to guide 15–17% FY27 revenue growth. [S5] [S4]
- **Export/institutional support:** Exports rose to 21% of revenue from 16% a year earlier, while FII and DII ownership increased year-on-year in the June 2026 shareholding filing. [S5] [S6]

## Bear Case
- **Margin pressure:** EBITDA margin fell 170 bps to 11.2%. Management attributes most of the decline to copper; price-variation clauses cover about two-thirds of contracts but reset with a 1–2 quarter lag, leaving near-term margin risk. [S5] [S4]
- **Working-capital risk:** Receivable days increased from 78 to 96, mainly because two large state-utility customers are paying more slowly. Management says there have been no defaults, but the trend is worth monitoring. [S4]
- **Tax/legal exposure:** An NSE disclosure reports a ₹46.3 crore GST demand including interest and penalty for FY2020-21 and FY2021-22. The company plans to appeal and says it expects no material operational/financial impact; that outcome is not yet resolved. [S1]
- **Historical governance signal:** A 2024 news report said promoter pledge had risen to 35% of promoter holdings. However, the June 2026 NSE filing reports only 4.1% pledged, so the historical concern should not be treated as the current level. [S7] [S6]

## Open Questions
- Can copper-cost pass-through fully restore margins, and when will the 1–2 quarter lag unwind? [S4]
- What is the eventual cash/tax impact and probability of success of the ₹46.3 crore GST appeal? [S1]
- Will Bharuch start commercial production on schedule and ramp as planned? The supplied documents provide guidance, not actual commissioning evidence. [S5]
- The pack contains an unrelated article about a local cable-TV operator called “Sarvottam Cable Network”; it should not be attributed to NSE: SRVCABLE. [S2]
